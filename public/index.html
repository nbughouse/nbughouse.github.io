Bughouse N Player

Multiplayer bughouse chess with multiple simultaneous boards. Coordinate with teammates, pass captured pieces, and keep every board moving.

Start a match

  1. Enter a name.
  2. Create a new match or join with a 4-character match ID.
  3. Pick open board seats from the player plaques.
  4. The host starts the room once players are set.

During play

  • Captured pieces go to your teammate's pocket.
  • Drop pocket pieces onto eligible boards.
  • Matches end by checkmate, resignation, or timeout.
